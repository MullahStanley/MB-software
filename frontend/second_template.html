MORE THAN A MOMENT OF SUPPORT

Mental wellness deserves more than a single point of access.

For many people, mental wellness support is something they only think about when life becomes difficult.

But wellbeing is shaped by much more than one moment.

It's shaped by our relationships, our environments, our communities, our opportunities and the support available to us.

Mental Beacon is building a more connected approach—one that makes mental wellness part of the way people live, learn, work, relate and grow.

We believe support should be easier to find, easier to understand and more connected to the realities of everyday life.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

Mental wellness, connected.

We're building an ecosystem that brings together different ways of accessing mental wellness support, across the people and environments that shape our lives.

Our work is guided by three connected ideas.

Support that meets people where they are

Mental wellness support should be accessible through different pathways, depending on what people need and what works for them.

Environments that make wellbeing possible

We recognise that people don't live in isolation. Our homes, schools, campuses, workplaces and communities all influence our mental wellbeing.

Infrastructure that connects the experience

We're building the services, systems and experiences that help make mental wellness support more accessible, consistent and connected.

One vision. Different pathways. A more connected approach to mental wellness.

PEOPLE FIRST

Because real life doesn't happen in isolation.

Mental wellness is shaped by the lives we live and the environments we move through.

We're especially interested in the experiences of young adults navigating identity, relationships, education, work, family expectations and the uncertainty of becoming.

But our vision extends beyond individuals. We believe mental wellness support should also be accessible through the communities and institutions that shape people's everyday lives.

We want to help build a world where accessing mental wellness support feels more natural, more informed and less out of reach.

A SPACE FOR YOU

Sometimes, it helps to have someone who can help you make sense of things.

Counselling offers an opportunity to pause and explore what you're experiencing in a supportive, professional setting.

At MB Care, sessions are centred around your experiences, concerns and goals.

You may be navigating a particular challenge, trying to understand a recurring pattern, working through a relationship experience or simply wanting to develop greater self-awareness.

Whatever brings you to counselling, the process begins with understanding you.

WHAT BRINGS PEOPLE TO COUNSELLING?

Your experience doesn't have to fit into one category.

People seek counselling for different reasons. Some are working through a specific challenge. Others want support in understanding themselves and their lives more clearly.

Topics may include:

  • Stress and emotional overwhelm
  • Anxiety and worry
  • Relationships and communication
  • Boundaries and self-worth
  • Life transitions and uncertainty
  • Family and interpersonal challenges
  • Personal growth and self-understanding

You don't need to know exactly what to say before your first session. You can begin with what feels important to you.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions to have.

Still wondering about something? Write to us at therapy@mentalbeacon.co.ke.

Do I need to be experiencing a serious mental health problem to attend counselling?

No. People seek counselling for many different reasons, including stress, relationships, life transitions, emotional challenges and personal growth.

Is counselling confidential?

Counselling is conducted with respect for your privacy. Your counsellor will explain confidentiality and its professional and legal limits before or during your initial session.

What if I don't know what to talk about?

That's completely okay. Your counsellor can help you begin and explore what feels important to you.

Can I attend counselling virtually?

Yes. MB Care offers virtual individual counselling.

How much does a session cost?

Virtual individual counselling costs KES 2,500 per session. Physical individual counselling costs KES 3,000 per session.

How do I pay?

Virtual sessions require full payment before confirmation. Physical sessions require a 50% deposit, with the remaining balance payable after the session.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Please refer to our rescheduling and cancellation policy before completing your booking.

How do I book?

Choose your preferred session, select an available appointment, complete the short booking form and follow the payment instructions.
