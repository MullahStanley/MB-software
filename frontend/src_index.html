Our Programs

Built for young people.
Designed for real life.

Our programs go where young people are — into their schools, their stages of life, and their communities. Each one is intentional. Each one is designed to create lasting change.

Active Program

Little Beacons

Our preventive mental health programme for primary school learners (Grades 1–7). We believe that catching children before the hard seasons is the most powerful intervention of all. Age-appropriate, engaging sessions help children build emotional vocabulary, healthy coping skills, and inner confidence early — laying the groundwork for resilient young adults. We also equip teachers with practical tools to recognise and support struggling learners.

🌱 40+ students · 8 teachers reached

Affordable Therapy Access Program

Professional support shouldn’t be out of reach.

We believe every young person deserves access to a qualified therapist — regardless of what’s in their pocket. Through partnerships with vetted, licensed therapists, we connect young people to one-on-one mental health support at a subsidized rate of Ksh 1,500 per session — making professional care genuinely within reach.

1
You reach out to us through any of our channels.
2
We match you with the right therapist for your needs.
3
You access professional support at a rate that works for you.

Experiences We’ve Created

Showing up,
again and again.

From university campuses to primary schools, from virtual webinars to in-person gatherings — every event is a chance to remind a young person that they matter.

Wellness Experience · July 2026

Move & Reset Experience

Movement, laughter, reflection, and connection. Our first Move & Reset brought young people together for katabox, aerobics, and mental health reflection — a reminder that caring for your mind doesn’t always begin with words. Sometimes, it begins with showing up and moving your body.

University Partnership · June 2026

KCA University Mental Health Week

Mental Beacon facilitated programming across KCA University’s Mental Health Week, reaching 170+ students through honest conversations on consent, boundaries, and self-respect under the theme “Jipende.”

School Outreach · June 2026

Little Beacons — First School Visit

Our inaugural Little Beacons outreach brought preventive mental wellness conversations to primary learners, reaching 40+ students and equipping 8 teachers with practical wellbeing tools.

Outdoor Experience · April 2026

Mental Hangout at Oloolua Nature Trail

Step outside, breathe deep, and just be. We swapped screen time for green time — a fun outdoor hangout where good company, fresh air, and good vibes were the only agenda. Nature as therapy. Community as healing.

Community Experience · January 2026

Vision Boarding Picnic

We opened 2026 with intention. A guided reflection led by our founder, vision board creation, open conversations, games, and a shared picnic experience — supporting young people to think clearly about their personal growth and goals for the year ahead.

Community Outreach Initiative · November 2025

Ruiru Children’s Home — Community Outreach

Mental Beacon spent a day with children at Ruiru Children’s Home — creating a joyful, safe space filled with honest conversations about emotions, confidence, and self-worth. Every child left feeling seen, supported, and valued. Healing begins with presence.

Workshop · September 2025

It Starts With You — A Self-Love Edition

Our first physical mental health workshop invited young people to pause, reflect, and explore identity, self-worth, confidence, and the barriers that hold us back from loving ourselves fully. A room full of courage, vulnerability, and renewed belief.

Monthly Webinars · Ongoing

Youth Wellness Webinar Series

Our monthly webinars have reached 200+ young people with practical conversations on mental health and resilience. Free. Virtual. Open to all. Community built one session at a time.
